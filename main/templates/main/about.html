About Hotel Palav

Hospitality that feels like home — refined, relaxing, and unforgettable.

Who We Are

Established in 1998, Hotel Palav blends traditional hospitality with modern luxury in the heart of the city. Whether for leisure or business, our guests enjoy elegant rooms, fine dining, and world-class service.

Over two decades of excellence have made us a trusted name for comfort, care, and style. Our guests are our family — and every stay is crafted to be memorable.
